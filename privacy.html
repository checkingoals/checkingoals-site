Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026 · Applies to the current test version of Check In: Goals Together for iPhone

The short version: the goals, check-ins and notes you add stay on your iPhone. We don't have accounts, servers, ads, analytics or tracking in this version of the app.

Who we are

Check In: Goals Together ("Check In", "we", "us") is an iPhone app made by Monica Chitre. If you have questions about this policy, email support@checkingoals.com.

Information you add to the app

When you use Check In you may add your first name, goals (titles, descriptions, schedules, check-in times, reminder settings and your "why"), and check-ins with optional notes. This information is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we can't see it.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by the app on your iPhone. They don't go through a server. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in iPhone Settings.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account, email address or phone number to use the app.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't sell or share your information.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Testing through TestFlight

If you test the app through Apple's TestFlight, Apple provides us with some information about your testing, such as the name and email address you were invited with (not shown for public-link testers), your device model, iOS version and language, install and session counts, and crash reports. If you choose to send feedback, Apple also shares your comments and screenshots and some technical details about your device. Apple handles this information under its own policy: see TestFlight & Privacy. We use it only to improve the app.

Emailing us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to improve the app, and we'll delete it if you ask.

Keeping or deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting a goal removes it and its check-ins. Deleting the app removes all of your information from your iPhone. If your iPhone backups include app data, your goals may also be in those backups, which you control.

Children

Check In isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

Future versions may add features such as accounts or goals with a buddy, which would involve storing some information on a server. We'll update this policy before any such feature is released, and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@checkingoals.com.

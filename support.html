How check-ins work

Check in on time

Every goal has a check-in time. Tap Check in before it and your streak grows by a day.

Running late

After the check-in time you can still check in until midnight, with a short note on what happened. Your streak keeps going, marked late.

Can't make it

Cancel before the check-in time and say why. Your streak pauses for the day, up to 2 times a week per goal. After that, a cancel counts as a missed day.

Missed

No check-in by midnight counts as missed, and your streak starts again.

Reminders

Each goal can remind you 30 minutes before (the default), at the check-in time, 1 hour before, or not at all. You can also get one late nudge an hour after the check-in time. Late nudges aren't sent between 10 PM and 7 AM.

Not getting reminders? Open Check In and go to Profile › Notifications, then tap Turn on. You can also check iPhone Settings › Notifications › Check In. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, so open the app at least every few days to keep them up to date.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. The current version doesn't have accounts or send your goals anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the Check In app from your iPhone. That removes all of your goals, check-ins and notes.

Can I change a goal?

Yes. Open the goal, tap ⋯, then Edit goal. You can also undo today's check-in from the same menu.

How do I send feedback during testing?

Take a screenshot in the app, tap the preview, then Done › Share Beta Feedback. Or open Profile › Send feedback.
